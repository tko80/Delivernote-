Manual Backup & Restore

Your notes and signatures are stored on this iPhone. Export a backup file regularly and keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a backup merges notes by ID and does not erase existing notes.

Tip: Save the exported file to Files, iCloud Drive, or another safe location. This app does not upload your data to a server.

Data safety

Deleting Safari website data or removing the installed web app may remove locally stored records. Export backups often. Keep this app installed and open it online occasionally so its offline files remain available.
